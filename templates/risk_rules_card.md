# Risk rules card

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Risk per trade | 1% to 3% of bankroll |
| Related positions | 20% of bankroll at most on outcomes that lose together |
| Minimum edge | 5 points between my probability and the price |
| Minimum price | 15 cents |
| Order type | Limit orders. Market orders only to exit a position that has gone wrong |
| Monthly stop | Down 15% in a month: stop until next month, review the log |
| Hard stop | Down 40% from deposits: stop. Do not add money to recover |
| Adding money | Only after passing a 100-trade block, never to cover losses |

Size by bankroll:

| Bankroll | 1% | 2% | 3% |
|---|---|---|---|
| $500 | $5 | $10 | $15 |
| $2,500 | $25 | $50 | $75 |
| $5,000 | $50 | $100 | $150 |
| $10,000 | $100 | $200 | $300 |
| $25,000 | $250 | $500 | $750 |

Never trade: combos and parlays, live sports, 15-minute crypto, perpetual futures.
